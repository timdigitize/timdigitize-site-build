About Tim

We make digital farming possible for everyone. In every field, in every language, at every budget.

Why we exist

Because digital farming is everyone's right — not the privilege of a few brands. Three barriers still stand in the way.

01

Out-of-reach pricing

Precision farming technology sits above the budget of most farmers in the world.

02

Closed systems

Brand-to-brand incompatibility locks the farmer into a single ecosystem.

03

Non-localized support

Native-language documentation and native-language technical support are still the exception, not the rule.

We exist to remove those barriers — to bring digital farming to the geographies that actually need it, and to lift the quality of life of every stakeholder from field to table.

Milestones

How we got here

  1. 1988

    Heritage begins

    Timsan Tarım A.Ş. is founded to manufacture precision spray nozzles.

  2. 1994

    International expansion

    Representation and distribution begin for foreign brands in agricultural spraying machines, systems and parts.

  3. 2021

    Digital transformation

    R&D work begins on digital farming systems.

  4. 2022

    Production line

    An electronics production line is built to manufacture our own digital farming systems in-house.

  5. 2023

    Aqua & Hydra series

    Aqua-series liquid control boxes and Hydra-series hydraulic control boxes are developed.

  6. 2024

    Nova 100 & 110

    Nova 100 and Nova 110 rate control computers are developed.

  7. 2025

    Nova 200 & TimApp

    Nova 200 section control computer is developed. TimApp — the platform farmers use to manage their operations and Tim equipment — continues to evolve.

  8. 2026

    Tim Dijital Teknolojiler A.Ş.

    To sharpen focus, the business moved to a group structure: Tim Dijital Teknolojiler A.Ş. was founded to develop and grow Tim's digital farming technologies under one roof.

  9. 2026

    Nova 130

    Nova 130 rate control device for fertilizer spreaders is developed.

How we work

We listen from the field, we build for the field.

01

Listening

Every product starts in the field. We listen to the farmer, understand what they actually need, and only then sit down to design.

02

Design

What we hear becomes a sketch. We design real solutions to real problems.

03

Development

Software and hardware advance together. No product is finalized without being tested in the field.

04

Manufacturing

Every product is built in our own facilities. We don't rebadge other brands — from design to production, everything comes from our own hands.

What we don't believe

Four things we refuse to accept

1 — Technology is not a privilege

We don't believe digital farming belongs only to big farms, big budgets and big markets.

2 — A farmer should not be locked in

We don't believe that buying a product should make you a captive of that brand. Your equipment is yours — and the choice should be yours too.

3 — Technology should not be incomprehensible

We don't believe a farmer should need to be an engineer to use technology. Technology you can't use isn't technology.

4 — Distance is not silence

We don't believe it's normal to leave a farmer alone after the sale. The sale is the beginning, not the end.
