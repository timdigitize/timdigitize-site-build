Hydra 500 AIR

5 Way Hydraulic Control Box · AIR ECU Wireless

Hydra 500 AIR gives you leveling, height, lock and 2 boom movements from the cab, while the solenoid directional valves stay connected on the sprayer. The SIM109 valve cable plugs into socket 4 of the AIR ECU; box and ECU communicate wirelessly, so no valve cable runs through the cab.

💡 Best for: wide-boom field sprayers that are frequently uncoupled from the tractor.
Contact us Request a quote Download Catalog

Overview

Hydra 500 AIR is a wireless 5-way hydraulic boom control box: the switch panel stays in the cab, the solenoid directional valves stay on the sprayer, and the AIR ECU links the two without a cable through the cab. It controls leveling, height, lock and 2 boom movements and shares its ECU with the Aqua AIR section control boxes.

Frequently asked questions

Hydra 500 AIR — Frequently asked questions

Which boom movements does Hydra 500 AIR control?

Leveling, height and lock plus 2 boom movements, 5 functions in total, each with its own switch. The SIM109 cable connects the TIMFAYP16 solenoid valve block to socket 4 of the AIR ECU on the sprayer.

Can I retrofit Hydra 500 AIR to my existing field sprayer?

Yes. Hydra 500 AIR is designed as a retrofit kit: the computer or box goes in the cab, the TIMFAYP16 electro valve kit is fitted to the sprayer plumbing and the valve cable connects the two. Any brand of field sprayer with a standard valve block can be converted, usually in one working day by your dealer.

Do I need a second ECU if I already have Aqua AIR?

No. Hydra 500 AIR plugs into socket 4 of the same AIR ECU that drives the Aqua 500 AIR or Aqua 700 AIR valves, so one ECU on the sprayer handles both section control and hydraulics.

How much does Hydra 500 AIR cost and how do I buy it?

The price depends on the valve kit, cable lengths and options (GNSS antenna, sensors) your machine needs, so we quote per configuration. Use the "Request a quote" button on this page or write to us on WhatsApp; a Tim dealer in your country will reply with a complete kit price and delivery time.

What support and warranty come with Hydra 500 AIR?

Every Tim product ships with a 2-year warranty, a printed quick guide and access to the Nova Support wizard in the Field Hub. Installation and operator training are handled by the local dealer; firmware updates are free and loaded via USB.
