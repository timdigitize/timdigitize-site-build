Nova 210

Orchard Navigation Control Computer

Nova 210 brings the Nova 200 workflow to orchards and vineyards. 2 or 4 sections switch automatically from the field map and GPS position, the target rate is held constant at every speed, and the orchard perimeter is recorded with GNSS so flow stops outside the boundary. A–B guidance runs along the tree rows.

💡 Best for: orchard and vineyard sprayers that want GNSS section control in a compact 2 / 4 section layout.
Contact us Request a quote Download Catalog

Overview

Nova 210 is a GPS spraying computer with automatic section control (ASC) for the orchard or vineyard sprayer. It replaces three devices in the cab: the rate controller, the GNSS guidance display and the section switch box. Using the Cross 200 or Cross 300 antenna, it draws the field on a colour screen, switches 2 / 4 boom sections on and off automatically on overlaps and headlands, holds the rate constant and records the field boundary, which cuts chemical use by 15 to 30 percent on irregular fields.

Key features

Key features

Auto Section Control

2 or 4 sections switch on/off from the field map and GPS position — no overlaps, no skips, less chemical waste.

A–B Guidance

Straight-line guidance along tree rows from two reference points for uniform passes.

Cross GNSS Options

Orchard Sprayers

Compact 2 / 4 section layout for orchard and vineyard sprayers — the Nova 200 workflow, sized for trees.

Frequently asked questions

Nova 210 — Frequently asked questions

What is automatic section control and how much chemical does it save?

Automatic section control closes each boom section the moment it enters an area that is already sprayed or lies outside the field boundary, and opens it again on new ground. Field measurements on irregular fields show 15 to 30 percent less chemical compared with manual switching, plus no missed strips.

Can I retrofit Nova 210 to my existing orchard or vineyard sprayer?

Yes. Nova 210 is designed as a retrofit kit: the computer or box goes in the cab, the 40 BAR 2W Orion / Wolf electro valve kit is fitted to the sprayer plumbing and the valve cable connects the two. Any brand of orchard or vineyard sprayer with a standard valve block can be converted, usually in one working day by your dealer.

Do I need RTK for Nova 210?

Not necessarily. With the standard Cross 200 antenna Nova 210 works at sub-meter accuracy, which is enough for section control on most booms. For centimetre-level guidance, narrow rows or automatic steering readiness, choose the Cross 300 Smart Antenna, which is RTK ready with a cellular correction service or base station.

How much does Nova 210 cost and how do I buy it?

The price depends on the valve kit, cable lengths and options (GNSS antenna, sensors) your machine needs, so we quote per configuration. Use the "Request a quote" button on this page or write to us on WhatsApp; a Tim dealer in your country will reply with a complete kit price and delivery time.

What support and warranty come with Nova 210?

Every Tim product ships with a 2-year warranty, a printed quick guide and access to the Nova Support wizard in the Field Hub. Installation and operator training are handled by the local dealer; firmware updates are free and loaded via USB.
