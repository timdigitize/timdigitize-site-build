Aqua 700

7 Way Control Box

Aqua 700 controls 7 section valves, the main valve and pressure regulation from the cab. Easy section control prevents pressure deviation when sections open or close, and no chemical hose is carried inside the cabin.

💡 Best for: wide-boom field sprayers with 7 sections and manual operation.
Contact us Request a quote Download Catalog

Overview

Aqua 700 is a 7-way sprayer control box (section switch box) for the field sprayer. From the cab the operator opens and closes 7 section valves and the main valve and adjusts the pressure, so no chemical hose enters the cabin. It is the simple, robust way to add electric section control to a mechanical sprayer, and it works with the 20 BAR 7W electro valve kit.

Frequently asked questions

Aqua 700 — Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a control box and a rate controller?

Aqua 700 is manual: the operator switches sections and adjusts pressure with toggle switches. A rate controller such as Nova 100 holds the l/ha rate automatically using sensors. Many farmers start with an Aqua box and move to a Nova computer later; the same valve kit stays on the sprayer.

Can I retrofit Aqua 700 to my existing field sprayer?

Yes. Aqua 700 is designed as a retrofit kit: the computer or box goes in the cab, the 20 BAR 7W electro valve kit is fitted to the sprayer plumbing and the valve cable connects the two. Any brand of field sprayer with a standard valve block can be converted, usually in one working day by your dealer.

Is there a wireless version of Aqua 700?

Yes. Aqua 700 AIR uses the AIR ECU wireless valve interface: the box stays in the cab, the valve harness plugs into the ECU on the sprayer and the two communicate wirelessly, with no valve cable through the cab.

What support and warranty come with Aqua 700?

Every Tim product ships with a 2-year warranty, a printed quick guide and access to the Nova Support wizard in the Field Hub. Installation and operator training are handled by the local dealer; firmware updates are free and loaded via USB.
